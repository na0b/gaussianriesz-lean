\begin{definition}[Multi-indices and their order]
Write $\mathbb{N}_0 = \{0,1,2,\ldots\}$, so that a multi-index is an element
$\alpha = (\alpha_1,\ldots,\alpha_n) \in \mathbb{N}_0^n$, i.e. a function
$\alpha : \{1,\ldots,n\} \to \mathbb{N}_0$.  Its order is the nonnegative integer
\[
  |\alpha| = \alpha_1 + \cdots + \alpha_n = \sum_{j=1}^n \alpha_j .
\]
Thus $|\alpha| = 0$ holds exactly for the zero multi-index $\alpha = 0$, and
$|\alpha| \geq 1$ for every $\alpha \neq 0$.  We write $e_j$ for the multi-index
whose $j$th entry is $1$ and whose other entries vanish, and $\alpha - e_j$ for
the multi-index obtained from $\alpha$ by decreasing its $j$th entry by one,
with the convention that this entry is left at $0$ when $\alpha_j = 0$.
\end{definition}
