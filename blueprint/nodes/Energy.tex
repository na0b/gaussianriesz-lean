\begin{definition}[The fractional energy and the form domain $\mathcal{V}$]
For $v \in L^2(\gamma_n)$ with Hermite coefficients $v_\alpha$ relative to
\noderef{HermiteBasis}, define the fractional energy as the value in
$[0,+\infty]$
\[
  \mathcal{E}(v) = \sum_{\alpha \neq 0} |\alpha|^{1/2}\, |v_\alpha|^2 ,
\]
with $|\alpha|$ as in \noderef{MultiIndexSum}.  All summands are nonnegative, so
the sum is unconditionally defined in $[0,+\infty]$; it is finite exactly when
$v$ lies in the fractional form domain
\[
  \mathcal{V} = \{v \in L^2(\gamma_n) : \mathcal{E}(v) < \infty\}
    = \operatorname{Dom}(L^{1/4}) ,
\]
the second equality being the case $s = 1/4$ of \noderef{FracPowDomain}, since
the multiplier there is $|\alpha|^{2\cdot(1/4)} = |\alpha|^{1/2}$.  Thus
$\mathcal{E}(v) = \|L^{1/4}v\|_2^2$ for $v \in \mathcal{V}$, and the convention
$\mathcal{E}(v) = +\infty$ for $v \in L^2(\gamma_n)\setminus\mathcal{V}$ is built
into the definition, as in the reference paper.  The space $\mathcal{V}$ carries
the Hilbert norm $\|v\|_{\mathcal{V}}^2 = \|v\|_2^2 + \mathcal{E}(v)$.  Constants
belong to $\mathcal{V}$ and have zero energy, since the index $\alpha = 0$ is
omitted from the sum; no mean-zero condition is imposed.
\end{definition}
