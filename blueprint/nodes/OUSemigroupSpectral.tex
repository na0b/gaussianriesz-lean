\begin{lemma}[The semigroup acts by the exponential multiplier]
For every $s \geq 0$, every $v \in L^2(\gamma_n)$ and every
$\alpha \in \mathbb{N}_0^n$, the element $P_s v$ of \noderef{OUSemigroup} has
Hermite coefficients
\[
  (P_s v)_\alpha = e^{-s|\alpha|}\, v_\alpha ,
\]
relative to the basis of \noderef{HermiteBasis}.  In particular the degenerate
convention in \noderef{OUSemigroup} is never in force for $s \geq 0$, and
$P_s h_\alpha = e^{-s|\alpha|}h_\alpha$, $P_s\mathbf{1} = \mathbf{1}$.
\end{lemma}
\begin{proof}
Let $s \geq 0$ and let $(v_\alpha)_\alpha$ be the coefficient family of $v$,
which is square summable with $\sum_\alpha |v_\alpha|^2 = \|v\|_2^2$ by Parseval
(\noderef{HermiteBasis}).  Since $|\alpha| \geq 0$ and $s \geq 0$, we have
$-s|\alpha| \leq 0$, hence $0 < e^{-s|\alpha|} \leq 1$ for every $\alpha$.
Therefore
\[
  \sum_{\alpha} \bigl| e^{-s|\alpha|} v_\alpha\bigr|^2
    \leq \sum_\alpha |v_\alpha|^2 = \|v\|_2^2 < \infty ,
\]
so the multiplied family is square summable.  By \noderef{HermiteBasis} the
coefficient map is an isometric isomorphism onto $\ell^2(\mathbb{N}_0^n)$, so this
family is the coefficient family of a unique element of $L^2(\gamma_n)$, and by
the definition in \noderef{OUSemigroup} that element is $P_s v$.  Hence
$(P_s v)_\alpha = e^{-s|\alpha|}v_\alpha$ for every $\alpha$.

Taking $v = h_\beta$, whose coefficients are $1$ at $\alpha = \beta$ and $0$
elsewhere, gives $P_s h_\beta = e^{-s|\beta|}h_\beta$.  For $\beta = 0$ we get
$P_s h_0 = h_0$ since $|0| = 0$, and $h_0 = \mathbf{1}$ by
\noderef{Hermite1Zero}.
\end{proof}
