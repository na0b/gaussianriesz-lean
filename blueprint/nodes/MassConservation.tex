\begin{lemma}[Conservation of Gaussian mass]
Let $F \in L^2(\gamma_n)$ with $m = \int F \, d\gamma_n$, let $a \in \mathbb{R}$,
let $u \in L^2(\gamma_n)$, and suppose
$\eta_2 \in L^2(\gamma_n)$ represents the multiplier functional of
\noderef{ObstacleMultiplier} on $\mathcal{V}$.  Put $g = a\mathbf{1} - \eta_2$.
Then
\[
  \int g \, d\gamma_n = m ,
  \qquad\text{equivalently}\qquad
  \int \eta_2 \, d\gamma_n = a - m .
\]
\end{lemma}
\begin{proof}
The constant function $\mathbf{1} = h_0$ of \noderef{HermiteL2} and
\noderef{Hermite1Zero} is an admissible form-domain test function: by
\noderef{Energy} its energy is $0$, since the defining series omits the index
$\alpha = 0$ and all other coefficients of $h_0$ vanish; hence
$\mathbf{1} \in \mathcal{V}$.

Apply the representation hypothesis with $\varphi = \mathbf{1}$.  Its left-hand
side is, by \noderef{ObstacleMultiplier},
\[
  \mathcal{E}(u, \mathbf{1}) - \int (F - a)\, d\gamma_n .
\]
By \noderef{EnergyConstantInvariance} the first term is
$\mathcal{E}(u,\mathbf{1}) = 0$.  Hence the hypothesis reads
\[
  -\int (F-a)\, d\gamma_n = \int \eta_2 \, d\gamma_n ,
\]
all integrals being finite by \noderef{L2IntoL1} together with
$\gamma_n(\mathbb{R}^n) = 1$ (\noderef{GaussIsProb}).  Using
$\int F \, d\gamma_n = m$ and $\int a \, d\gamma_n = a$, the left-hand side is
$a - m$, so
\[
  \int \eta_2 \, d\gamma_n = a - m .
\]
Finally $\int g \, d\gamma_n = \int (a - \eta_2)\, d\gamma_n = a - (a-m) = m$.
\end{proof}
