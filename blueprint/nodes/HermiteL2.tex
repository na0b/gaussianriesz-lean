\begin{definition}[The Hermite family as elements of $L^2(\gamma_n)$]
By \noderef{HermiteFunMemLp} each Hermite function $h_\alpha$ of
\noderef{HermiteFun} is square integrable with respect to $\gamma_n$.  We write
again $h_\alpha \in L^2(\gamma_n)$ for the almost-everywhere equivalence class it
determines, so that
\[
  \{h_\alpha : \alpha \in \mathbb{N}_0^n\} \subseteq L^2(\gamma_n)
\]
is an $\mathbb{N}_0^n$-indexed family of vectors in the real Hilbert space
$L^2(\gamma_n)$, whose inner product is
$\langle v, w\rangle = \int v w \, d\gamma_n$.
\end{definition}
