\begin{definition}[The orthonormal Hermite basis of $L^2(\gamma_n)$]
By \noderef{HermiteL2Orthonormal} the family $\{h_\alpha\}_{\alpha \in \mathbb{N}_0^n}$
of \noderef{HermiteL2} is orthonormal in $L^2(\gamma_n)$, and by
\noderef{HermiteL2Complete} its closed linear span is all of $L^2(\gamma_n)$.
It is therefore an orthonormal basis of the real Hilbert space $L^2(\gamma_n)$,
indexed by $\mathbb{N}_0^n$.  We fix this basis once and for all and write
\[
  v_\alpha = \langle v, h_\alpha\rangle = \int v\, h_\alpha \, d\gamma_n,
  \qquad \alpha \in \mathbb{N}_0^n ,
\]
for the Hermite coefficients of $v \in L^2(\gamma_n)$.  Parseval's identity then
holds in the form $\|v\|_2^2 = \sum_{\alpha} |v_\alpha|^2$, the coefficient map
$v \mapsto (v_\alpha)_\alpha$ is an isometric isomorphism of $L^2(\gamma_n)$ onto
$\ell^2(\mathbb{N}_0^n)$, and every square-summable family of coefficients is
realized by a unique element of $L^2(\gamma_n)$.
\end{definition}
