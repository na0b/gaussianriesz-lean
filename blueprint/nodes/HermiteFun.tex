\begin{definition}[The $n$-dimensional Hermite functions]
For a multi-index $\alpha \in \mathbb{N}_0^n$ as in \noderef{MultiIndexSum}, define
$h_\alpha : \mathbb{R}^n \to \mathbb{R}$ as the product of the one-dimensional
normalized Hermite functions of \noderef{Hermite1} in the separate coordinates:
\[
  h_\alpha(x) = \prod_{j=1}^n h_{\alpha_j}(x_j),
  \qquad x = (x_1,\ldots,x_n) \in \mathbb{R}^n .
\]
Each $h_\alpha$ is a polynomial in $x$ of total degree $|\alpha|$.  This is the
Hermite family of the reference paper, built from the measure $\gamma_n$ of
\noderef{GaussMeasure}, which factorizes as the $n$-fold product of the
one-dimensional measure $\pi^{-1/2} e^{-t^2}\,dt$.
\end{definition}
