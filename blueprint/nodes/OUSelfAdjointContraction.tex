\begin{lemma}[Self-adjointness and $L^2$ contractivity of $P_s$]
For every $s \geq 0$ and all $v, w \in L^2(\gamma_n)$,
\[
  \langle P_s v, w\rangle = \langle v, P_s w\rangle
  \qquad\text{and}\qquad
  \|P_s v\|_{L^2(\gamma_n)} \leq \|v\|_{L^2(\gamma_n)} ,
\]
with $P_s$ as in \noderef{OUSemigroup}.
\end{lemma}
\begin{proof}
By \noderef{OUSemigroupSpectral} the coefficients of $P_s v$ and $P_s w$ are
$e^{-s|\alpha|}v_\alpha$ and $e^{-s|\alpha|}w_\alpha$.  Parseval's identity from
\noderef{HermiteBasis} applies to both inner products, giving
\[
  \langle P_s v, w\rangle = \sum_\alpha e^{-s|\alpha|} v_\alpha w_\alpha
    = \langle v, P_s w\rangle ,
\]
all three series being absolutely convergent by Cauchy--Schwarz in
$\ell^2(\mathbb{N}_0^n)$ together with $0 < e^{-s|\alpha|} \leq 1$ (valid because
$s \geq 0$ and $|\alpha| \geq 0$).  The two outer expressions coincide because the
middle series is symmetric in the roles of $v$ and $w$.

For the contraction, Parseval and the same bound $e^{-s|\alpha|} \leq 1$ give
\[
  \|P_s v\|_2^2 = \sum_\alpha e^{-2s|\alpha|}|v_\alpha|^2
    \leq \sum_\alpha |v_\alpha|^2 = \|v\|_2^2 ,
\]
and taking square roots finishes the proof.
\end{proof}
