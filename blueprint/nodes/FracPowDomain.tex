\begin{definition}[The spectral domains $\operatorname{Dom}(L^s)$]
Let $v \in L^2(\gamma_n)$ have Hermite coefficients
$v_\alpha = \langle v, h_\alpha\rangle$ relative to the basis of
\noderef{HermiteBasis}.  For $s \in \mathbb{R}$ set
\[
  \operatorname{Dom}(L^s)
    = \Bigl\{ v \in L^2(\gamma_n) :
        \sum_{\alpha \neq 0} |\alpha|^{2s}\,|v_\alpha|^2 < \infty \Bigr\},
\]
i.e. $v \in \operatorname{Dom}(L^s)$ exactly when the multiplied coefficient family
$m_s\bigl((v_\alpha)_\alpha\bigr)$ of \noderef{FracPowCoeff} is square summable.
This is the spectral domain criterion of the reference paper.  For $s \geq 0$ the
multiplier $|\alpha|^{2s}$ is at least $1$ on every nonconstant mode, while for
$s \leq 0$ it is at most $1$.  The two cases used below are $s = 1/2$, where
$\operatorname{Dom}(L^{1/2}) = \{v : \sum_{\alpha\neq0}|\alpha||v_\alpha|^2<\infty\}$
is the operator domain of $B = L^{1/2}$, and $s = 1/4$, whose domain is the
fractional form domain.
\end{definition}
