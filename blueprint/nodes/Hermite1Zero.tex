\begin{lemma}[The zeroth Hermite function is the constant one]
With $h_k$ as in \noderef{Hermite1}, one has $h_0(x) = 1$ for every
$x \in \mathbb{R}$.
\end{lemma}
\begin{proof}
By the defining recurrence recorded in \noderef{Hermite1}, $\mathrm{He}_0$ is the
constant polynomial $1$, so $\mathrm{He}_0(\sqrt2\,x) = 1$ for every
$x \in \mathbb{R}$.  Moreover $0! = 1$, hence $\sqrt{0!} = 1$.  Substituting both
into the formula of \noderef{Hermite1} gives
\[
  h_0(x) = \frac{\mathrm{He}_0(\sqrt2\,x)}{\sqrt{0!}} = \frac{1}{1} = 1 .
\]
\end{proof}
