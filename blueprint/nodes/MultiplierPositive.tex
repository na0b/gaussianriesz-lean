\begin{lemma}[Dual-order positivity of the multiplier]
Let $F \in L^2(\gamma_n)$, $a \in \mathbb{R}$, and let $u \in \mathcal{K}$ minimize
the functional $J$ of \noderef{ObstacleFunctional} over the cone $\mathcal{K}$ of
\noderef{ObstacleCone}.  Let $\eta$ be the multiplier of
\noderef{ObstacleMultiplier} associated with $u$, $F$ and $a$.  Then
\[
  \langle \eta, \varphi\rangle \geq 0
  \qquad\text{for every } \varphi \in \mathcal{K},
\]
i.e. $\eta$ takes nonnegative values on every nonnegative test function of finite
energy.  This is the statement $\eta \geq 0$ in the dual order; it is not yet a
pointwise inequality for a function.
\end{lemma}
\begin{proof}
Let $\varphi \in \mathcal{K}$, so $\varphi \geq 0$ $\gamma_n$-a.e.\ and
$\mathcal{E}(\varphi) < \infty$.  Put $v = u + \varphi$.  Then $v \geq 0$ a.e.,
because $u \geq 0$ and $\varphi \geq 0$ a.e., and $\mathcal{E}(v) < \infty$
because $\mathcal{V}$ is a linear subspace of $L^2(\gamma_n)$; hence
$v \in \mathcal{K}$ by \noderef{ObstacleCone}.

Apply \noderef{VariationalInequality} with this competitor $v$.  Since
$v - u = \varphi$, it gives
\[
  \mathcal{E}(u, \varphi) \;\geq\; \int (F-a)\,\varphi \, d\gamma_n .
\]
Rearranging,
\[
  \mathcal{E}(u,\varphi) - \int (F-a)\varphi\, d\gamma_n \;\geq\; 0 ,
\]
and by \noderef{ObstacleMultiplier} the left-hand side is exactly
$\langle\eta,\varphi\rangle$.
\end{proof}
