\title[Blueprint of the Lean formalization]{A direct obstacle proof of a dimension-free weak-type $(1,1)$ bound for the Gaussian Riesz transforms: blueprint of the Lean formalization}
\author{Cristian E. Guti\'errez, Juan J. Manfredi}

\begin{document}
\begin{abstract}
This document collects the natural-language statements and detailed proofs that accompany the Lean~4 formalization of the main results of C.~E.~Guti\'errez and J.~J.~Manfredi, \emph{A direct obstacle proof of a dimension-free weak-type $(1,1)$ bound for the Gaussian Riesz transforms} (arXiv:2610.26478).
Each subsection corresponds to the Lean declaration of the same name in the directory \texttt{GaussianRiesz/}; cross-references point to the results that a proof uses.
Section~\ref{sec:main} lists the formalized results of the paper: Theorem~3.1, Theorem~5.1, Proposition~5.3 and Corollaries~5.4 and~5.5.
Appendix~\ref{sec:deviation} records the one place where the formalization departs from the paper's argument: the proof of Proposition~5.3 does not assume the maximal estimate (5.15) of Fabes, Guti\'errez and Scotto, and proves a localized form of it instead.
Within each section, items appear in dependency order.
\end{abstract}
